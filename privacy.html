Embedded Video (YouTube)

Our home page includes a short video from YouTube, a Google service. We embed it in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), and the player loads only after you press play. Our cookie banner does not control YouTube's player, so if you prefer not to load it, you can watch the video on YouTube directly. Health Information & HIPAA Clarification

Important: Telehealth Navigator AI is not a covered entity under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We are an educational information service, not a healthcare provider, health plan, or healthcare clearinghouse. We do not collect, store, or process Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA.

Our free AI guide is designed for general educational conversations about telehealth options. It is not designed or intended for sharing personal health information, symptoms, diagnoses, or treatment details. We strongly advise against sharing any personal medical or mental health details through our chat or any other feature on this site.

If you are looking for a platform that complies with HIPAA for clinical conversations, we recommend connecting with a licensed telehealth provider directly. Our HIPAA and Telehealth Privacy Guide explains how HIPAA applies to telehealth services and what protections to look for when choosing a provider.

Data handling for chat: Chat messages sent through our AI feature are processed by OpenAI's API under their standard data processing terms. Each request carries your most recent messages and the chat's replies, up to 20 in all, plus any answers you gave to the optional personalization questions on the chat page. If any of your messages among them matches our crisis screen, nothing is sent to OpenAI and the chat shows crisis resources instead. Otherwise, our server code passes them to OpenAI without saving them or writing their text to our logs, and the conversation itself lives only in the open chat page, so it disappears when you close or reload it. However, we cannot guarantee how OpenAI handles data transmitted to their service. For this reason, do not enter any information you would consider private health data.

Data Retention

We keep no copy of chat conversations: they exist only in the open chat page in your browser until you close or reload it. To enforce chat rate limits (30 messages per 10 minutes and 100 per day), our server does keep request counts for each 10-minute window and each day, labelled with a hashed form of your IP address rather than the address itself. These counts hold no message text. Each one is deleted no later than about 2 days after its 10-minute window or day began: an hourly cleanup removes any count that old, and many are removed sooner, when your next window starts. However, messages sent to OpenAI for processing may be retained by OpenAI according to their data retention policies.
