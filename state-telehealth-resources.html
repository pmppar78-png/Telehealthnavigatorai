PSYPACT and state psychology boards

The map above is a general access snapshot. This table is the licensing reference: whether PSYPACT lets a qualifying psychologist practice telepsychology into that jurisdiction, and the psychology board that actually licenses psychologists there. It was checked on October 22, 2026.

PSYPACT (the Psychology Interjurisdictional Compact) is not a license to practice in every state. A psychologist still needs a home-state license, plus an Authorization to Practice Interjurisdictional Telepsychology (APIT) for video care or a Temporary Authorization to Practice (TAP) for short in-person work. Both the home state and the state where the client is located have to be effective PSYPACT jurisdictions. Counselors, social workers, and prescribers do not practice under PSYPACT. Confirm the current map at psypact.gov before you rely on a row, because legislatures are still moving.

How this table was sourced

  • Effective means the jurisdiction appears as a telepsychology destination on the PSYPACT Commission snapshot dated February 24, 2026 (APIT holders practicing into that state). The commission map itself is an image. The state list used here is that snapshot, published as the PSYPACT Snapshots PDF of February 24, 2026. Forty-two jurisdictions in the 50 states and D.C. are marked effective. The Northern Mariana Islands are also on that snapshot and are not a row in this table.
  • Alaska enacted PSYPACT on June 25, 2026 (PSYPACT notice). That notice does not state an effective date, so Alaska is not marked effective.
  • Iowa enacted PSYPACT on July 1, 2026, and the same notice says it is effective one year later, July 1, 2027 (PSYPACT notice).
  • Board names and websites are the psychology licensing boards listed by the Association of State and Provincial Psychology Boards on its Contact a Licensing Board directory, retrieved October 22, 2026. Use the board site to verify a license. ASPPB is the directory; the board is the regulator.

Controlled-substance prescribing by telemedicine

DEA tele-prescribing update (checked October 22, 2026): DEA and HHS extended the COVID-19 telemedicine flexibilities for prescribing controlled medications through December 31, 2026. Under 21 CFR 1307.41, a DEA-registered practitioner may prescribe Schedule II–V controlled substances via telemedicine without having conducted an in-person medical evaluation, through the end of that day, if the prescription is for a legitimate medical purpose, uses an interactive telecommunications system, the prescriber is authorized to prescribe that class, and the rest of 21 CFR Part 1306 is met (Federal Register, Dec. 31, 2025; DEA). A state may set a stricter rule. Check back in January 2027. This extension ends at the close of December 31, 2026, and the rule that replaces it is not set. This note is educational, not prescribing advice. See also telehealth laws by state and care across state lines.

About This Data

Access ratings are based on a composite assessment of multiple factors including: state telehealth parity laws, Medicaid reimbursement policies, licensure requirements, audio-only coverage, originating site requirements, and overall regulatory environment for telehealth services.

Data is compiled from publicly available sources including state legislation, Medicaid agency publications, and policy reports. Telehealth policies change frequently; this information reflects general patterns and may not reflect the most recent legislative changes. Always verify current policies with your insurance provider and state regulatory agencies.

Important Information

This resource provides general educational information about how telehealth rules and coverage differ from state to state. It does not constitute legal, medical, or insurance advice. Telehealth policies and regulations change frequently. Coverage availability depends on your specific insurance plan, provider, and circumstances. Always verify current policies and coverage with your insurance company and healthcare providers before making decisions about your care.
