Lisa Lewis, RN, BSN

About Lisa

Lisa Lewis, RN, BSN is a Registered Nurse with a Bachelor of Science in Nursing and more than 25 years of progressive nursing experience. She currently serves as a Nurse Manager in a forensic mental health locked unit, leading care for acute behavioral health patients in one of the most clinically demanding inpatient settings in U.S. healthcare.

Lisa's career has focused on the places where nursing, mental health, and patient safety meet. She has worked across behavioral health and skilled nursing environments, with progressive leadership responsibility for staff supervision, regulatory compliance, care planning, and day-to-day risk management on units that serve patients in crisis or with severe and persistent mental illness.

Areas of expertise

  • Behavioral health operations and unit leadership
  • Crisis stabilization and acute mental health environments
  • Patient advocacy and patient dignity
  • Risk management and clinical safety
  • Care planning across multidisciplinary teams
  • Staff leadership, training, and supervision
  • Regulatory and survey compliance in inpatient settings
  • High-acuity mental health and forensic psychiatric care
  • Therapeutic patient engagement and de-escalation
  • Trauma-informed care

Professional philosophy

“I focus on providing a safe environment while maintaining a therapeutic relationship with patients experiencing acute crisis or severe mental illness, prioritizing stabilization, risk management, and patient dignity.”

— Lisa Lewis, RN, BSN

That orientation — safe environment, therapeutic relationship, patient dignity — is the lens she brings to reviewing telehealth and mental health content. Educational guidance for someone considering online therapy for the first time should sound like a calm, knowledgeable nurse: accurate, non-alarming, honest about limits, and clear about when to call 988 or 911.

What Lisa reviews on this site

Lisa supports Telehealth Navigator AI as an editorial reviewer for educational telehealth and mental health guidance. Her review focuses on:

  • Clarity and accuracy of safety language on YMYL ("your money or your life") health topics.
  • Crisis disclaimers, escalation paths (988, 911, local emergency services), and when in-person care is the right answer.
  • Trauma-informed, non-stigmatizing tone in condition explainers (anxiety, depression, panic, PTSD, OCD, and related topics).
  • Plain-language fidelity to publicly published clinical guidance (APA, NIMH, SAMHSA, CMS).
  • Patient dignity and privacy framing on platform comparisons, insurance pages, and "first session" guidance.

Reviewed pages carry a visible "Reviewed by Lisa Lewis, RN, BSN" line near the top and the corresponding reviewedBy field in their structured data. Reviewer scope & important limits

Reviewer scope is limited to educational content. Lisa Lewis reviews educational telehealth and mental health guidance for clarity, accuracy of safety language, and alignment with patient-centered, trauma-informed practice. Her review of an article on this site is not:

  • Personal medical advice for any individual reader.
  • A diagnosis, treatment plan, or care recommendation.
  • A prescription or any guidance on starting, stopping, or changing medication.
  • An emergency or crisis assessment.
  • An endorsement of any specific telehealth platform, therapist, or clinician.

Lisa's review of educational content does not create a nurse-patient relationship between her and any reader of this site. For care decisions, work with a licensed clinician in your state who can review your full history. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, text HOME to 741741, or call 911.
