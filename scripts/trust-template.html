988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org. It is free, confidential and open 24/7. Call 911 if you or someone else is in immediate danger. This page is educational and is not a crisis service.

When to seek emergency care: Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room if someone has tried to harm themselves, has a plan and the means to act on it, cannot stay safe, may have overdosed, or is confused, unresponsive or out of touch with what is real. Don't wait for a telehealth appointment or a reply to a portal message in any of these situations.
